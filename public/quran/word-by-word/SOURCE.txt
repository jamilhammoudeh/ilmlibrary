Word-by-word English translation of the Quran.

Translation: Dr. Shehnaz Shaikh and Ms. Kausar Khatri,
"The Glorious Quran Word-for-Word Translation".
The translators have made the text free for any halaal use,
including sale; attribution is shipped in-app as a courtesy.

Obtained via QUL / Quran.com (api.qurancdn.com), snapshotted in this
repo at assets/quran/word-by-word/. Regenerated for the web reader by
web/scripts/build-wbw-assets.mjs, which splits two entries (8:6, 13:37)
so every word's position matches the QPC v4 mushaf layout glyphs 1:1.
